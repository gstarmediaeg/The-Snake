About Me

THESNAKE
Hossam Mohamed

I'm Hossam Mohamed, formerly known as Thesnake, a multidisciplinary visual artist and designer currently working under the name The Snake. Over the years, he has explored a wide range of artistic disciplines, including 3D art, visual design, digital illustration, and creative direction.

His work blends different styles and techniques, allowing him to develop a distinctive artistic identity. Throughout his journey, The Snake has collaborated on numerous projects with artists from the rap and Shaabi music scenes, as well as with producers and creatives from various backgrounds. Driven by experimentation and a passion for visual storytelling, he continues to expand his craft and push creative boundaries across multiple mediums, constantly seeking new ways to transform ideas into impactful visual experiences.
